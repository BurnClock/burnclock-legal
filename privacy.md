# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aman Kumar (operating as BurnClock) ("I", "me", "my") collects,
uses, and protects your information when you use BurnClock ("the App").
By using the App, you agree to the practices described
here.

---

## 1. Information I Collect

### Information you provide directly
- **Account information:** name and email address when you create an account
- **Personal details:** date of birth, biological sex, height, and body
  weight — used to calculate your nutrition and fitness targets
- **Health and fitness data:** body fat percentage, workout logs (exercises,
  sets, reps, weight lifted), meal logs (foods eaten, calories, macros),
  and daily step counts
- **Goal information:** your target weight, pace preference, and activity
  level

### Information collected automatically
- **Usage data:** which features you use and when, to improve the App
- **Device information:** device type and operating system version, for
  compatibility purposes

### Information from third parties
- If you sign in with Apple or Google, I receive your name and email
  address from those services, subject to their privacy policies

---

## 2. How I Use Your Information

I use your information solely to:
- Calculate your personalised calorie targets, macro goals, and body
  composition projections
- Power the AI-assisted meal logging feature (food photos and text
  descriptions are sent to Google's Gemini AI service for analysis)
- Show you your progress over time through the body avatar and trend graphs
- Send you reminder notifications if you enable them
- Maintain and improve the App

**I do not sell your personal data.** I do not share your health or fitness
data with advertisers.

---

## 3. Health and Body Data — Special Notice

This App collects sensitive health-related information including body
weight, body fat estimates, and dietary intake. This data is used
exclusively to power the App's features for your personal benefit.

**Important limitations:** The App provides estimates and projections based
on established scientific formulas. It is not a medical device and does not
provide medical advice. Body fat percentage figures are labeled "projected"
because they are model-based estimates, not clinical measurements. Consult
a healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet or
exercise regime.

---

## 4. AI-Assisted Meal Analysis

When you photograph or describe a meal:
- The image or text is sent to Google's Gemini AI service via an encrypted
  connection
- Nutrition data is cross-referenced against the USDA FoodData Central
  database
- Barcode scans are looked up against the Open Food Facts database
- Images are compressed before transmission and are not stored permanently
  by the AI service

Meal photos you choose to save are stored in your account on Supabase
(our backend provider) in a private, access-controlled folder.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. Data in transit is encrypted
using TLS. Access to your data is restricted by Row Level Security — no
other user can access your data, and I access it only for support purposes
with your permission.

The App also stores data locally on your device for offline use. This
local data is protected by your device's own security.

## 7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete
your account at any time from the Profile tab → Account → Delete Account.
Account deletion permanently removes all your data from our servers within
30 days. Local data on your device is erased immediately on deletion.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years of age. The onboarding
flow blocks sign-up for users who indicate they are under 13. If you
believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please
contact me at the address below and I will delete it promptly.
